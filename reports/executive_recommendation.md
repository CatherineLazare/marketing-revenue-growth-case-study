# Executive recommendation: recover funnel revenue in 90 days

## Recommendation

Prioritize fixing the MQL-to-SQL handoff before increasing acquisition spend. The data indicates that quality and response speed—not lead volume alone—are the immediate constraints. Focus first on paid social and webinar conversion, while enforcing a 24-hour SLA for high-intent MQLs.

## 90-day plan

| Timing | Action | Owner | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Define shared MQL/SQL criteria, audit paid-social audiences, and introduce response-time alerts. | Marketing Ops + Sales Ops | 95% of high-score MQLs contacted within 24 hours |
| Days 31–60 | Launch two paid-social audience tests; add behavior-based webinar scoring and an SDR follow-up sequence. | Demand Gen + SDR leadership | +5 percentage points in paid-social MQL-to-SQL conversion |
| Days 61–90 | Scale the winning audience, retire weak creative, and review channel economics in a weekly funnel meeting. | VP Marketing | Pipeline gain and conversion lift sustained for four weeks |

## Investment and governance

Keep the test budget within the existing paid-social allocation; reallocate only after a test beats the control on SQL rate and cost per SQL. Publish a weekly scorecard covering lead volume, MQL-to-SQL rate, response-SLA compliance, pipeline ARR, and revenue at risk. Marketing and sales should jointly own the handoff metric.

## Decision requested

Approve a 90-day funnel recovery sprint with Marketing Operations as the accountable owner, an SDR SLA sponsor, and a weekly executive review. Do not scale paid-social spend until the qualified-pipeline test has demonstrated improvement.
